## Event Overview

Explore how data becomes art together. One parent/guardian and one child will use math to find patterns in a dataset, then use those patterns to drive servo motors and create an artistic representation of relationships in the data.

- **Date:** November 19, 2026
- **Time:** 4:30–6:30 PM PST
- **Duration:** 2 hours
- **Location:** HDSI Lab 3.0, UC San Diego
- **Participants:** Each spot is a pair of people: one parent/guardian and one child.
- **Capacity:** 4 pairs per session. Check Eventbrite for current availability.
- **Cost:** $40 per pair. Enter promo code **lab3** on Eventbrite for **50% off**, bringing the price to **$20 per pair**.

## Activities

- Find patterns and relationships in a dataset
- Connect mathematical patterns to servo motor movements
- Create robotic art that expresses relationships in the data
