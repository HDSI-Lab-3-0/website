# Parent/Guardian and Child Robotic Arts & Crafts Workshop

## Summary

1 parent/guardian and 1 child work together to experience learning circuits, Arduino basics coding, and robotics through creative making.

## K-12 Learning Goals

Build confidence in circuits + programming; connect robotics to data science foundations.

## Undergraduates Learning

Facilitation, teaching, kit setup, troubleshooting, and curriculum refinement.

## Overview

This workshop creates an opportunity for parents/guardians to work with their kids to learn about basic programming, circuits, and robotics. One parent/guardian and one child come to our lab and enjoy a 2-hour experience. Light snacks provided. Parents/guardians work with their child to learn about basic programming with an Arduino, basic circuits, and robotics. We share how programming is one of the fundamental skills for data science.

## Workshop Details

- **Duration**: 2 hours
- **Participants**: 1 parent/guardian + 1 child team
- **Age Range**: Grades 4-6
- **Location**: On-campus lab
- **Food**: light snacks provided
